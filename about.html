Our Story

TRIBS Logistics & Services Corp. started in 2004 in Las Piñas City with a simple idea: businesses need one partner they can count on to keep things moving.

What began as an automotive repair shop grew into a full logistics company. Today we repair vehicles, rent out trucks with experienced drivers, and deliver parcels and goods to the last mile, all under one roof.

Our clients range from small retailers and online sellers to distributors and contractors across Metro Manila and nearby provinces.

Our Mission

To provide dependable repair, transport and delivery services that keep our clients' businesses moving, through skilled people, a well-maintained fleet and a commitment to doing the job right the first time.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted one-stop logistics and service partner in the Philippines, known for reliability, integrity and on-time performance.
